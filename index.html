5 things you need to know about Open Access in the ERA

The quick version — before you dive into the detail below.

01

Strong policies drive success

Clear, well-funded open access policies work. Fragmented rules create confusion and slow progress.

02

Game-changing legal moves

Two big wins: EU-wide secondary publication rights and stronger research exceptions mean better access to publicly funded research.

03

Publishing is evolving

Traditional models still dominate, but community-led open access is a game-changer in the humanities and social sciences.

04

Support over compliance

Open science thrives when the focus is on people, not just rules. Training, guidance and investment make the difference.

05

Humans vs. bots

99.9% of open access traffic is generated by humans — but AI-driven scraping is rising fast. It's time to secure trust and sustainability.
